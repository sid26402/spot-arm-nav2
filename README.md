# Spot with arm: Nav2 + body/wrist-camera pick-and-place (Isaac Sim 6.0 / Isaac Lab 3.0)

Boston Dynamics Spot with arm, fully simulated in Isaac Lab 3.0 / Isaac Sim 6.0. Spot navigates a maze with ROS 2 Humble Nav2 (AMCL localisation on a simulated lidar, a moving obstacle that is not in the map). It identifies coloured cubes on a table with its front body camera, asks which cube to pick and which bin to put it in, localises the chosen cube with the wrist camera, picks it with a reinforcement-learning arm policy, carries it through the maze and places it in the bin.

Demo video: (link coming)

## What it does
Spot walks to a table with Nav2 (2D lidar from PhysX ray casts + map), its front **body camera** identifies the coloured cubes,
the operator chooses a cube and a bin (panel, keyboard or `--order red:bin2,blue`), Spot walks to that cube, the **wrist camera**
measures it to ~1 mm, an RL arm policy picks it, and Spot carries it to the chosen bin - repeating until no cubes are left.
Missed grasps are detected by the wrist camera; rotated/tipped cubes are skipped.

## Architecture
```
+------------------------------- Isaac Lab app (full_demo_main.py) -------------------------------+
|  walking policy (RL)   arm policy v9g (RL)   body camera   wrist camera   simulated 2D lidar    |
|  task flow: Nav2 leg -> body-camera look -> ask -> approach -> look -> pick -> carry -> place    |
+---------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------^-------------+
                | UDP 9870-9873: odometry, lidar scan, goals            velocity cmds |
                v                                                                    |
+--------------- spot_ros_relay.py / goal_bridge.py  (ROS 2 Humble) -------------------+-------------+
|  /odom  /scan  /tf  ->  Nav2 (map_server, AMCL, planner, controller, costmaps)  ->  /cmd_vel      |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## Requirements
- Ubuntu 22.04, NVIDIA RTX GPU, Isaac Sim 6.0 + Isaac Lab 3.0, ROS 2 Humble with Nav2.

```bash

ISAACLAB_DIR=~/IsaacLab SPOT_ASSETS=~/spot_assets ./install.sh
```

## Run
```bash
./run_demo.sh --scene pick_test --multi_flow --body_cam                 # short scene, choose cubes in the panel
./run_demo.sh --scene default --multi_flow --body_cam --order red:bin2,blue   # zig-zag course, automatic answers
```

## How it works
- `full_demo_main.py` - the demo (generated by `build_demo.sh` from `full_demo_perc.py` + `build/` patches).
- `nav/` - world/map, Nav2 config, UDP relay, goal bridge, stand-pose planner.
- `isaaclab_ext/` - custom Isaac Lab tasks (walking policy env, arm lift env).
- `policies/` - exported walking policy (`spot_arm_flat_v2`) and arm policy (`spot_arm_topdown_v9g`).

## Known limits
- Simulation only. Object masks come from the simulator's instance segmentation (a real robot needs a detector).
- Arm policy first-try grasp rate: 12/12 with perception v8.24 in the full flow (see Results); misses are caught by the wrist camera and retried or skipped.
- The walking policy cannot make small sideways steps; the planner works around it (turn-walk-turn, steer-in approach).

## Results (measured in the full flow)

| Perception version | Scene | Localisation | Runs | Cubes placed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v8.18 (grasp gate on) | course | exact odometry | 2 | 4/6 |
| v8.22 (grasp gate on) | course | exact odometry | 2 | 3/6 |
| **v8.24 (shipped)** | course | exact odometry | 2 | **6/6** |
| **v8.24 (shipped)** | pick_test | exact odometry | 1 | **3/3** |
| **v8.24 (shipped)** | course | **AMCL + drifting odometry** | 1 | **3/3** |

With v8.24, every first grasp succeeded (12/12), with 0 false grasps and 0 knocked cubes. Nav2 reached every goal in every run, with no aborts.
In the AMCL run, odometry drifted by up to 3.9 m / 60°. Nav2 still arrived within 3–5 cm of its table goals.
Twelve picks is a small sample: the real success rate is high, but not guaranteed to be 100%.

## What comes from sensors and what is simplified

**From sensors**
- Body camera: identifies the cubes by colour and locates them (1–2 cm).
- Wrist camera: measures the chosen cube's position and angle (~1 mm). The arm policy receives this camera estimate, not the simulator's cube position.
- Grasp verification: wrist camera plus finger joint angle.
- Simulated 2D lidar: PhysX ray casts against the scene colliders. Nav2 uses it for its costmap and, with `--amcl`, for localisation.
- Walking and arm control: proprioception.

**Simplified (simulation shortcuts)**
- The last ~0.5 m of docking at the table and the bins uses the ground-truth robot pose. Nav2 itself uses AMCL with `--amcl`.
- Object segmentation masks come from the renderer (a stand-in for a trained detector).
- Table and bin positions come from a known map. A real robot would map the area first.
- The moving obstacle is added to the lidar scan geometrically from its live pose, because GPU PhysX ray casts don't detect kinematically moved bodies. Nav2 still only sees it as scan points; it is not in the map.
- The "cube in bin" score is measured with simulator truth (evaluation only).

## Known limitations
- When two cubes go to the same bin, the second can land on the first and slide (it has always stayed inside the bin so far).
- The arm policy was trained on square cubes. A cube turned more than about 15° is skipped.

## Repository layout

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `full_demo_perc.py` | **Perception + pick/place** (v8.24): body-camera cube detection, wrist-camera localisation, grasp verification, place logic. Runs standalone too |
| `full_demo_main.py` | The full demo: `full_demo_perc.py` + the Nav2/flow patches, generated by `build_demo.sh` |
| `build_demo.sh`, `build/` | Patch chain that regenerates `full_demo_main.py` from `full_demo_perc.py` |
| `nav/` | Nav2 side: world/scene definitions, map generation, simulated lidar, ROS 2 relay and goal bridge, AMCL parameters, `nav_up.sh` |
| `isaaclab_ext/` | Isaac Lab task configs (Spot-arm locomotion and lift) and the play script; `install.sh` links them into Isaac Lab |
| `policies/` | Trained policies: walking `spot_arm_flat_v2`, arm `spot_arm_topdown_v9g` |
| `assets/` | Scripts that recreate the modified NVIDIA robot and bin USDs (the USDs themselves can't be redistributed) |
| `install.sh`, `run_demo.sh` | Set up paths on your machine; start the demo |
